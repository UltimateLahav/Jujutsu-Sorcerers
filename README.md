# Jujutsu Sorcerers

Landscape pixel-art mobile game prototype.

- **Title screen:** Gojo lifts his blindfold and his one glowing eye blinks (4 closing frames, held shut for 0.5 s, then the same frames in reverse).
- **Character select** (Gojo + locked slots), then he spawns in standing on a **zebra crossing** at night (see **The map** below). Gojo is drawn in side view, facing right, in the same detailed 64x64 16-bit style throughout: a 6-frame wind idle (12 FPS, frames per second) and separate 15-frame walk-forward and walk-backward cycles (15 FPS, so one full cycle takes a second and he takes one step every half second; the walking speed is matched to that stride so his feet do not skate).
- **Wind and movement:** a headwind blows right-to-left. His hair stands upright with only a tiny tip flutter, his blindfold tails sway a little, and his lower clothes stay still; walking changes his stride and arm swing.
- **Jump:** a 15-frame jump at 15 FPS (frames per second, so exactly one second): he crouches ("bucks") to push off, tucks his knees up to his crotch/waist at the top, then reaches down, lands on his toes and absorbs the landing before standing up again.
- **Double jump (teleport up):** tap **BLUE** while he is in the air and he collapses into blue Six Eyes light, flashes into place about 34 art pixels up, then drops and lands hard. 15 more frames at 20 FPS (0.75 s). Only one teleport per jump.
- **While jumping:** you can steer left/right in the air (including through the teleport and the fall), but not while he crouches or lands; presses during those moments, or while he is still spawning in, are ignored. The ground shadow shrinks while he is up.
- **Controls:** a **joystick** at the bottom-left (it replaces the old BACK / FWD / JUMP / CROUCH buttons). Push it **left / right** to walk backward / forward, **up** to jump (one jump per push, even if you keep holding it) and **down** to crouch (he stays crouched for as long as you hold it; let go and he stands up). Diagonals combine: up-right is a jump while walking forward, down-right is the crouch-walk, and a quick down-then-up flick is the higher spring jump out of the crouch. Up and down only count when the stick points clearly up or down (30+ degrees off sideways), so a thumb that drifts a little while walking does not jump or crouch. The bottom-right has five small buttons: the **MODE** button on its own at the top, right above BLUE (tap it to go through the modes: off, cursed energy, red), then **BLOCK** and **BLUE** (Lapse Blue), then **DASH** and **ATTACK** below; BLUE in mid-air is the teleport up. You can hold the stick with one thumb and use the buttons with the other. On a keyboard: arrow keys or A / D to walk, Up / W / Space to jump (Up again in the air also teleports), S / Down / C to toggle the crouch, J / F to attack, K or Shift to dash, hold B to block, L for BLUE (RED in red mode), E for the mode button. The city scrolls with parallax.

- **Attack combo:** the ATTACK button (bottom-right; keyboard J or F) throws a 4-hit combo, one hit per press, 15 frames per hit: **1** right hook, **2** left hook, **3** right elbow (blue glow on the hand) and **4** roundhouse kick. Every attack is blue now (the kick used to be red): the hitting hands and legs glow blue and have a blue outline. The button shows which hit is next. Press again while a swing is still running and the next hit starts the moment it ends; wait longer than about 1.4 s and the combo starts over at the right hook. Hits 1-3 run at 30 FPS (half a second each), the kick at 24 FPS. He stands his ground while attacking, attacks work on the ground only, and each hit has its own whoosh, a deep impact thump and a tiny screen shake.
- **Sound** (all synthesised in code by `tools/sfx.js`, no audio files; the sound starts after the first tap): leather-on-concrete **footsteps** locked to the walk animation (one step every half second, exactly when a foot lands, alternating feet, slightly different every time; a calm, low heel-then-toe thump rather than a sharp click), a very quiet looping **wind** (a little stronger when he walks into it), and deep **jump**, **landing** and **teleport** sounds (charge, vanish, arrive, fall, heavy landing). The jump / landing / teleport sounds keep their deep bass at normal strength while the air and noise parts are softened. A small room reverb gives them space. The **SND ON/OFF** button in the game bar mutes everything (remembered), and the Android app silences the game while it is in the background. Levels live in the `LEVEL` constants at the top of `tools/sfx.js` (rebuild with `tools/build_html.py`).

- **Settings** (the SET button in the game bar, next to SND): a volume slider from 0 to 200% (100% = the designed level, saved on the device) for MASTER, FOOTSTEPS, WIND, JUMP + LANDING, TELE + BLUE (teleport, Lapse Blue and Max Blue), ATTACKS and CURSED ENERGY. Let go of a slider and you hear that sound (the wind is already playing underneath). The game ignores the controls while the settings are open; Escape / the Android back button closes them.
- **Training dummy:** SPAWN DUMMY in the settings puts an inverted-colour Gojo in front of him, facing him (REMOVE DUMMY or going back to the menu clears him). A hit that reaches him (the hooks and elbow about 15-17 px from Gojo's hips, the kick 26 px) flashes him white, pops up a damage number, plays a body-blow sound and shoves him back (the kick hardest); a swing that falls short just whiffs, so walk up to him first. He has a health bar (300, with a pale trail showing the damage just taken); at zero he is knocked out (topples over, flickers, fades) and a fresh one steps in after a moment.
- **Hit counter and combo meter** (top right): HITS and total damage while you keep landing hits (it restarts after 1.6 s without one), and a 4-pip meter that fills while the hits land in order right hook > left hook > elbow > kick. All four in order adds 25 bonus damage, shows COMBO! and plays a finisher sound. A KO counter sits underneath.
- **Hit reactions:** ten reactions of ten frames each - head snap, doubled over, stagger back, full spin, knocked flat (and gets up), skid on the heels, arms up to guard, hop back, dizzy wobble and drop to one knee - picked at random every time a hit lands, on the dummy *and* on Gojo. They are one set of art in Gojo's own colours, reused inverted for the dummy.
- **Attacking dummy:** SPAWN ATTACKER in the settings (replacing the punching bag) makes the inverted Gojo walk up to you, swing random hits from the combo (with inverted glows: the blue becomes orange) and rest between them. When a swing connects you reel with a random reaction, get knocked back, cannot act until you recover and are untouchable for a moment afterwards. Jumping or teleporting out of the way dodges it. You can fight back: he flinches, can be knocked out, and a new attacker steps in.

- **Lapse Blue** (the BLUE button above-left of ATTACK; keyboard L): *standing*, he blinks forward - folds into blue light, streaks across and re-forms right in front of the enemy (50 px ahead if there is nobody). *Holding the stick to the right (forward) while you press it*, he stands his ground instead: one hand comes out of his pocket, an orb of blue light gathers, and he pulls the enemy in (a quarter second) and stuns it for 0.4 s. Each is 15 frames; there is a short cooldown.
- **Knockback:** hooks and the elbow only nudge the enemy (about 1-2 px) so the whole combo keeps connecting; the roundhouse kick sends him sliding about 15 px with a dust trail along the ground.
- **Crouch** (pull the joystick down; keyboard S / Down / C toggles it): a **deep crouch** (the hips drop 15 px, so his head is about 14 px lower than before) and **nothing can hit him while he is down**, not even the low kick (the attacker's swings miss; he is also safe while going down and standing up). Every crouch animation is **15 frames**: going down (stand-up is the same frames backwards, 0.42 s), the idle (the legs never move, only the head sways), crouch-walk forward and back (60% speed, softer steps), the spring jump out of the crouch (about 30 px high), the crouch punches and the crouch dash. Blocking works crouched too, and Max Blue from a crouch is unchanged.
- **Crouch punching:** the ATTACK button works while crouched too (the button label changes to R.JAB / L.UPPR). Two 15-frame punches alternate, one per press: a low right jab (about 10 damage) and a left uppercut that rises from below (about 13 damage), both at 30 FPS. They reach about 16 px, only nudge the enemy, count as hits on the hit counter, and he stays crouched and in place while throwing them. Press twice quickly to chain them; wait 1.4 s and the combo starts again with the jab.
- **Max Blue** (press BLUE / keyboard L *while crouched*): he stands up first (the 10 crouch frames backwards), then summons **Max Blue** with a 40-frame animation at 20 FPS (2 seconds): the hand comes out of his pocket, a small orb of blue light grows above his palm while a deep-blue outline glows around him, he braces and channels, then releases it.
  - **The orb** is a big glowing sphere in pixel art (a 15-frame loop played at 20 FPS): a white-hot core with wispy veins of light, mid-blue clouds that keep boiling, a dark ragged rim and thin cyan arcs swaying along the edge. It floats at head height in front of him and lives for 3.4 seconds. It dims the sky, drags the wind and about 70 chunks of rooftop debris into itself (the far half of the swirl passes behind the orb, the near half in front, with short motion streaks, glowing blue as they get caught), sends rings across the floor and rumbles the screen faintly.
  - **The enemy is caught by it:** he is lifted off the floor and swung round the orb, tumbling, passing behind it and then in front of it, on a loop that keeps tightening (about 50 px away at first, 28 px once he is deep in) and gets faster, so he is moved around *and* pulled in. He can't be hit while he is caught and his AI is held; the orb grinds him down (about 4-6 damage every quarter second, but it can never finish him). When the orb collapses, the blast flings him off, straightening him up in the air, he lands about 85 px in front of Gojo, takes 25 more damage and plays a hit reaction (and goes down if that was the last of his health).
  - Jump, attack, crouch and Lapse Blue are ignored while he summons it, there is a short cooldown afterwards, you can't start a second orb while one is out, and he can still move and fight while it is. Getting hit during the summon cancels it. Sound: a rising swell, a deep boom as it is released, a humming pull for as long as it lives, and a heavy blast at the end.

- **Dash** (the DASH button next to ATTACK; keyboard K or Shift): an evasive dash of 15 frames at 30 FPS (half a second). He dashes the way you are walking: hold the stick forward and he dashes forward (50 px), hold it back and he dashes back (42 px); standing still is a dash back (the evasive one). It works from the ground, **from a crouch** (the held direction decides and he stays crouched afterwards, with its own low dash animation) and **in mid-air** (jump, push the stick to the side you want, then press DASH; one air dash per jump, and the jump carries on underneath). The 15 frames are a low lunge forward or a leaning-back hop, with blue afterimages and speed streaks behind him and the wind rushing past; he is not hit while the dash is under way (about its first 0.37 s), and attacks, jumps, BLUE and crouching are ignored until it ends. It has a short dust puff on the ground and its own whoosh (on the JUMP + LANDING volume slider).

- **Block** (hold the BLOCK button, top-left of the button block; keyboard: hold B): his signature pose in the same side-view pixel style - the near hand comes out of the pocket and rises in front of his face with the index and middle fingers pointing up, while the other hand stays in his pocket (10 frames: 5 to raise it, 5 for the held pose, and it goes down again the same way when you let go). There is no visible barrier. While the pose is up, the attacking enemy's swings from the **front** are blocked (a BLOCKED popup, a bump of the screen and a small push back, no damage and no reaction); a swing from **behind** him still lands (dash past the attacker and he turns round and hits you in the back). He can **walk, crouch and crouch-walk while blocking**, but he is much slower (30% of his normal speed: about 13 px/s forward, 9 back; crouched about 8 px/s) and has his own 15-frame walking and crouching block animations with the raised hand; attacking, dashing, BLUE and jumping are ignored until you let go, and he cannot block in the air. If he is standing still the hand lowers smoothly when you let go; if he is moving or crouched it just drops.
- **Blocking dummy:** SPAWN BLOCKER in the settings puts an inverted-colour Gojo in front of you in the same raised-hand pose. **Normal punches** (the whole 4-hit combo and the crouch punches) are blocked: no damage, no hit counted, a BLOCKED popup. **Lapse Blue's pull is blockable but still works**: he is dragged to you but is not stunned. **Max Blue is unblockable**: he is caught by the orb and ground down as usual. A punch that reaches him from behind (his guard faces Gojo) lands normally, and he drops his guard while he is reeling from a hit.
- **Buttons:** the five action buttons are smaller than before (about 59 px, ATTACK 66 px, on a 390 px tall phone); the joystick kept its size.

- **Modes** (the **MODE** button right above BLUE, which shows OFF, CURSED or RED; keyboard E): each tap goes to the next mode, normal > **cursed energy** > **red** > normal. It is always off when you enter the game, and getting hit does not change it. **Cursed energy** (the first tap): unstable cyan flames burn on both of his hands. They are a 15-frame animation at 20 FPS (frames per second, the pictures shown each second) drawn after the reference picture: electric cyan with a bright glassy rim, a dark teal middle, a ragged black outline, black cracks and flying sparks. Every frame is a different random shape (a tall flame, a lash to the side, a compact lump) and the whole thing also shakes by a pixel and flickers, so it never holds still. While it is on:
  - **His hands come out of the pockets.** Every pose he can be in has a hands-out version (idle, both walks, the crouch and its walks, jump, teleport, dashes, block, Lapse Blue, Max Blue and the crouch punches): the near hand hangs forward, the far hand trails behind, the arms swing against the legs when he walks, and each fist is wrapped in the flames. The normal combo and the hit reactions keep the pockets.
  - **ATTACK always throws the 4-hit combo** (this replaces the old "walking forward" trigger; walking forward without cursed energy is the normal combo again), 15 frames per hit, each with a small lunge forward (never through the enemy): **1** a quick straight jab with the near hand, **2** a kick forward with the near leg, **3** a straight punch with the other hand, **4** a kick with the other leg (the hardest hit, it sends the enemy sliding with a dust trail). The button names the next hit (JAB, KICK, CROSS, KICK2). All four count towards the combo meter in order (the finisher bonus works the same), they are blocked by the blocking dummy like any normal punch, and the hitting hands and legs have a blue glow and a blue outline. Crouched, ATTACK is still the crouch jab and uppercut.
  - **Every hit he lands does 1.3 times its damage** (the finisher bonus and Max Blue's grind and blast too); the damage numbers turn cyan.
  - **Sound:** a power-up sound when it switches on (a swell and an ignition crack), a quiet random crackle while it burns, a power-down sound when it switches off, and its own volume slider in the settings.
  - **How it is made:** `tools/ce.py` draws the 15 flame frames; `tools/side.py` draws the hands-out poses (stored in `side.json` as the changed pixels on top of the normal frames, so the file only grew by about 10%) and records where each fist is on every frame, so the flames follow the hands exactly; the game draws the flames over the sprite and builds the hands-out sprites in the background right after start-up.

- **Red mode** (the second tap of the MODE button): there is **no cursed energy**: no flames and no crackle, instead **the outline of his hands is red** (the fists, and the raised two-finger hand), and the glow and outline on his punches and kicks turn red too. His hands stay out of the pockets, and there is no damage bonus. The **BLUE button becomes RED**, so Lapse Blue, Max Blue and the teleport up are not available until you switch back to normal.
  - **The red combo** (ATTACK; the button names the next hit): **1 NECK** a high kick to the neck with the near leg, **2 GUT** a kick to the stomach with the other leg, **3 PUNCH** a straight punch, **4 SMASH** a hard punch with a long wind-up that sends the enemy sliding back (about 25 px, with dust). 15 frames each, the same small lunge as the cursed-energy combo, and the hits count towards the combo meter and the finisher bonus in order.
  - **RED** (the red button; keyboard L; standing only): a remastered version of his block pose (the hand comes up with two fingers raised) while a **red orb charges for 20 frames (1 second)**, then he snaps his hand forward and fires it (30 frames in all). The orb flies forward leaving a glowing red trail and **explodes on the enemy** (a 15-frame blast, a screen shake and a split-second hit-stop, then a hard shove): 55-64 damage. The blocking dummy can block it (BLOCKED, no damage). Getting hit while it charges cancels it.
  - **Slow red, and the blue coat:** press RED while **walking backwards** (the stick or arrow held back) and the orb flies slowly (70 px/s, four times slower than normal). While it flies, tap the MODE button to go back to normal mode, then hold back and tap **BLUE**: the orb gets a **blue outline** and leaves a blue trail, Gojo leaps onto it, stands on it and is carried to the enemy. Where it touches him it bursts in a smaller blast: 30-37 damage and a **0.6 second stun** (he flashes blue and is not shoved away) while Gojo drops off right next to him (he is back in control after 0.22 s), so you can hit him. The blocking dummy can block it. A normal-speed orb cannot be coated.
  - The red look is made in the game by swapping the blue sprite colours (and the outline colour of the hands-out hands, which has its own palette letter) for red, so the hands-out poses and attacks needed no extra copies; `tools/side.py` draws the red combo and the RED pose (already with the hands out) and `build_html.py` ships them.

- **The map** (a street crossing at night, drawn in the same pixel style, replacing the old rooftop): he stands on a **zebra crossing**: white bars (a slanted bar every 14 px) on dark asphalt that scroll past under his feet as he walks and stay still when he does not. Across the road is a far sidewalk and kerb, and behind it the shops of a busy city: glass stores with brightly lit floors and warm shop fronts, a brick building with a pointed roof, tall vertical signs and bare trees, with skyscrapers full of lit windows behind them (they scroll at different speeds for depth) under a deep-blue night sky that glows purple above the rooftops. Every 120 px along the far sidewalk stands a **traffic light** or a **street lamp**: the traffic lights keep changing (4 s green, 1 s yellow, 4 s red, each pole on its own timing) with a soft glow, the lamps shine warm. The picture is drawn in code (`buildBg`, `buildFar`, `buildMid`, `buildGround`, `drawProps` in `tools/template.html`), so there are no image files.

- **Bystanders** (v3.1, redrawn and given voices in v3.2, put in two lanes in v3.3): five ordinary people (a businessman with a briefcase, an office worker with a bag, an old man with a cane, a jogger, a tourist with a backpack) walk the crossing. Each is a **64x64 pixel-art** figure in Gojo's 16-bit look (dark outline, four tones per colour, light from the upper right), drawn at start-up by `tools/human.js`: a little skeleton (hip, knees, ankles, shoulders, elbows) drives an 8-frame walk cycle and an 8-frame run cycle, and every body part is a shaded capsule or ellipse, so the proportions, shoulders, knees and shoes look like a real person; each also has a face (eye, brow, nose, mouth, ear), hair / cap / glasses and a **scared face** (wide eyes, open mouth) for the shock and run poses. **Nobody runs until somebody is killed.** They walk (each at their own speed) and ignore Gojo, even when he walks into them or attacks. **One hit kills**: every swing that lands (all combos, crouch punches), the **red orb** blast and **Max Blue** burst whoever is in reach. A kill is very gory: the body bursts into 29 pieces (the head cut from the real sprite, torso, arms, legs in the person's own clothes, heart, lungs, liver, stomach, intestines, ribs, flesh, bones) with torn red ends, ~90 blood drops, a fountain from the stump and a flash; pieces tumble, bounce and lie flat. **The blood stays**: pools and spatter are fixed to the road (they scroll with the crossing; capped at 450 spots, 150 loose pieces and 24 pools, the oldest are replaced). On each kill the game freezes for ~90 ms (hit-stop). **Panic:** everybody within 120 px of the kill freezes in **shock** for ~0.5 s (hands up, scared face, staring at Gojo), screams, then runs away from Gojo for ~8 s (the old man is slower than Gojo's walk, the jogger much faster) and calms down again. **Screams** (`scream` in `tools/sfx.js`): every person has their own voice (deeper man, higher woman, shaky old man, young jogger, tourist), the witnesses scream once and keep yelling while they run, and the victim gets one short cut-off yell right before the wet *splat*. A couple of seconds after a kill a new, calm person walks in from the edge of the screen (always five). No kill counter. Automated browsers (`navigator.webdriver`) get no bystanders unless a test sets `window.__pedsOn`, so the older regression suites are unchanged; `gtest22` covers this feature. The code is in `tools/human.js` (art), `tools/template.html` (`updatePeds`, `scarePeds`, `killPed`, `updateGore`, `drawBlood`) and `tools/sfx.js` (`scream`, `splat`).

- **Lanes** (v3.3): the zebra crossing is 12 px taller (it reaches further back than Gojo's lane). Gojo stands in the **middle lane**; the five people walk in an **upper lane behind him** (feet 4-10 px above his, drawn behind him) and a **lower lane in front of him** (feet 8-15 px below his, drawn in front of him), 3 and 2 of them. Hits, orbs and blasts reach both lanes, and blood and body parts land in the lane of the person who died.

- **Max Blue and the purple orb** (v3.3): Max Blue now stays up **1 second longer** (4.4 s instead of 3.4 s) so there is time to switch to red mode and fire RED into it. **RED fired into the Max Blue orb fuses both into a purple orb**: it hovers where the blue orb was, **shakes violently** (the shake grows as the fuse runs down), crackles with violet lightning (with red and blue flecks), and has **100 HP**. Swings from Gojo and from the enemy both damage it (their normal damage numbers). If its HP runs out before **1.4 seconds** it breaks without exploding; otherwise it **explodes: the enemy within 70 px loses 50% of his current health** and bystanders in the blast burst. The blast freezes the game for 150 ms (hit-stop) with a big screen shake; sounds: a rising violet hum during the fuse and a huge boom. Code: `fusePurple`, `damagePurple`, `explodePurple`, `drawPurple` in `tools/template.html`, `purple` / `purpleboom` in `tools/sfx.js`; `gtest23` covers it.

- **Domain Expansion: Infinite Void** (v3.4): a **DOMAIN** button (an eye) sits right next to the MODE button (key **V**); it only starts while Gojo stands still. **1.** Gojo raises his hand into the block pose and holds its **last frame**; the people and the enemy freeze. **2.** A **16-bit pixel-art version of the reference GIF** plays: the face behind the hand sign with glowing eyes, then a slow pan across the eyes. It is **64 pixels tall and 160 wide, one shared palette of 16 colours (two of them bright cyan for the glowing eyes) with a light ordered dither**, 40 frames at 8 FPS, drawn at 2x (320 x 128 game pixels) so it **fills the whole screen** (made by `tools/domain_art.py` from the GIF, which is kept outside the repo as `art/domain_reference.gif`, and embedded as `tools/domain_cut.png`, 45 KB). Black cinema bars frame the wide shot and the burst. **3.** The wide shot: the crossing with everybody standing still while the lights fail. **4.** A white burst (rays, shock rings, flash) opens the domain with the title DOMAIN EXPANSION and a hard-edged circle of void grows out of it. **5.** The **Infinite Void** (10 s): a **16-colour, 160 x 64 pixel-art recreation of the reference picture of the domain** (a glowing blue ring around a black hole with black blots, white ink splatters in the corners, a misty band and sparkles on a dark navy sky; the picture is kept outside the repo as `art/domain_look_reference.jpg`, `tools/void_art.py` makes `tools/void_cut.png`, 29 KB), drawn at 2x over the whole screen as the backdrop, in 12 frames: the ring turns (its blots go round with it), the glow of the splatters breathes and the sparkles flicker; blue ripples on the floor, the title INFINITE VOID; Gojo gets control back, **the enemy stays frozen** and **everything hits twice as hard**; the people stay frozen. **6.** The void collapses back to the crossing (the people and the enemy move again) and the button cools down for **30 s** (a fill counting down on the button). The domain has its own sounds (`domainstart`, `domainface`, `domainzoom`, `domainburst`, a drifting drone `voidon` / `voidoff` with random pings, `domainend`) in `tools/sfx.js`; `gtest24` covers it. Code: `startDomain`, `domTick`, `drawVoid`, `domVoidLayer`, `domOverlay` in `tools/template.html`.

- **Versus mode** (v3.7): a **VERSUS MODE** button on the select screen opens the setup screen: **your fighter** (Gojo) and the **enemy fighter** (Gojo with inverted colours), each in a row with locked slots left for more characters, and **six grades**: GRADE 4, GRADE 3, GRADE 2, SEMI-GRADE 1, GRADE 1, SPECIAL GRADE. Only Grade 4 is open at first; **beating a grade unlocks the next** (and your best stars per grade) and this is remembered on the device (`localStorage`: `jsv_unlock`, `jsv_stars`). A match is **best of 3 rounds** with health bars for both (Gojo 300 HP, the enemy 300 x the grade's health factor), the round points, a **60 s round timer** (when it runs out, whoever has more health wins; a tie goes to the enemy), ROUND n / FIGHT! / K.O. / TIME! banners, and a result screen with **1-3 stars** (70% / 35% of Gojo's health left in the deciding round) and NEXT GRADE / REMATCH / MENU. Everything you can do in the training game works (Max Blue, red, the purple orb, the domain, ...); the bystanders stay away. **The grade table** (`GRADES` in `tools/template.html`): health x0.6 / 0.8 / 1.0 / 1.3 / 1.7 / 2.3, damage x0.5 / 0.7 / 0.9 / 1.1 / 1.3 / 1.6, speed x0.75 ... 1.25, reaction time 700 / 520 / 380 / 260 / 160 / 90 ms, aggression, block and dodge chances, combos of 1-4 hits. **The AI** (`updateFighterVs`): after its reaction time it steps back out of your swing (a dodge, brief invulnerability) or raises its guard (a block, answered by a counter from Grade 2 up); from **Grade 2** it reads habits (a move you repeat is blocked more and more), does not start its own swing while yours is coming (it defends first, and trades if the defence fails) and drops a combo when you swing into it; from **Grade 1** it **punishes every miss** (it strikes in the 0.45 s after your swing whiffs); **Special Grade** also keeps its distance just outside your reach and baits, sidesteps red orbs and walks out of Max Blue and the purple orb. The SET menu hides the spawn buttons during a match. Tests: `gtest25`.

- **A death ends the round** (v3.8): whenever one fighter dies in a match the round ends **at once**, the other fighter **gets the point** and the next round starts by itself 2.5 s later with both fighters at full health and fresh positions (still best of 3; a time-out gives the round to whoever has more health). It works for **every way of finishing the enemy**: punches and kicks (fixed in v3.8: in v3.7 a normal blow that finished the enemy was never reported to the match, so no point was given and nothing reset), RED, Max Blue's blast, the purple orb and the domain's double damage; and for Gojo being finished by the enemy. A K.O. is counted only once. What you see: the picture freezes for 0.45 s on the final blow, then **the loser topples backwards** (the enemy fades away after a moment, Gojo stays down until the next round and then steps back in), the **K.O.** (or **TIME!**) banner shows with **YOU GET THE POINT** / **ENEMY GETS THE POINT** under the fighters, and the point that was just won **blinks** in the squares above the two health bars (they moved there from the middle of the screen, where the banners covered them). Two layout fixes with it: in a match the hit counter now sits at the left under Gojo's name (it used to cover the enemy's bar and name), and the new round's enemy steps in clear of the touch buttons. | Path | What it is |
|---|---|
| `index.html` | The whole game, one file, works offline in any browser |

**Install on Android:** copy the APK to the phone, open it, and allow "install unknown apps" if asked.
The APK uses a throwaway debug key (`android/debug.keystore`); publishing to Google Play needs your own release key.
