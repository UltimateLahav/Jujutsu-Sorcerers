# Jujutsu Sorcerers

Landscape pixel-art mobile game prototype.

- **Title screen:** Gojo lifts his blindfold and his one glowing eye blinks (4 closing frames, held shut for 0.5 s, then the same frames in reverse).
- **Character select** (Gojo + locked slots), then he spawns in on a rooftop. Gojo is drawn in side view, facing right, in the same detailed 64x64 16-bit style throughout: a 6-frame wind idle (12 FPS, frames per second) and separate 15-frame walk-forward and walk-backward cycles (15 FPS, so one full cycle takes a second and he takes one step every half second; the walking speed is matched to that stride so his feet do not skate).
- **Wind and movement:** a headwind blows right-to-left. His hair stands upright with only a tiny tip flutter, his blindfold tails sway a little, and his lower clothes stay still; walking changes his stride and arm swing.
- **Jump:** a 15-frame jump at 15 FPS (frames per second, so exactly one second): he crouches ("bucks") to push off, tucks his knees up to his crotch/waist at the top, then reaches down, lands on his toes and absorbs the landing before standing up again.
- **Double jump (teleport up):** tap **BLUE** while he is in the air and he collapses into blue Six Eyes light, flashes into place about 34 art pixels up, then drops and lands hard. 15 more frames at 20 FPS (0.75 s). Only one teleport per jump.
- **While jumping:** you can steer left/right in the air (including through the teleport and the fall), but not while he crouches or lands; presses during those moments, or while he is still spawning in, are ignored. The ground shadow shrinks while he is up.
- **Controls:** a **joystick** at the bottom-left (it replaces the old BACK / FWD / JUMP / CROUCH buttons). Push it **left / right** to walk backward / forward, **up** to jump (one jump per push, even if you keep holding it) and **down** to crouch (he stays crouched for as long as you hold it; let go and he stands up). Diagonals combine: up-right is a jump while walking forward, down-right is the crouch-walk, and a quick down-then-up flick is the higher spring jump out of the crouch. Up and down only count when the stick points clearly up or down (30+ degrees off sideways), so a thumb that drifts a little while walking does not jump or crouch. The bottom-right has four small buttons in a 2 x 2 block: **BLOCK** and **BLUE** (Lapse Blue) on top, **DASH** and **ATTACK** below; BLUE in mid-air is the teleport up. You can hold the stick with one thumb and use the buttons with the other. On a keyboard: arrow keys or A / D to walk, Up / W / Space to jump (Up again in the air also teleports), S / Down / C to toggle the crouch, J / F to attack, K or Shift to dash, hold B to block, L for BLUE. The city scrolls with parallax.

- **Attack combo:** the ATTACK button (bottom-right; keyboard J or F) throws a 4-hit combo, one hit per press, 15 frames per hit: **1** right hook, **2** left hook, **3** right elbow (blue glow on the hand) and **4** roundhouse kick. Every attack is blue now (the kick used to be red): the hitting hands and legs glow blue and have a blue outline. The button shows which hit is next. Press again while a swing is still running and the next hit starts the moment it ends; wait longer than about 1.4 s and the combo starts over at the right hook. Hits 1-3 run at 30 FPS (half a second each), the kick at 24 FPS. He stands his ground while attacking, attacks work on the ground only, and each hit has its own whoosh, a deep impact thump and a tiny screen shake.
- **Sound** (all synthesised in code by `tools/sfx.js`, no audio files; the sound starts after the first tap): leather-on-concrete **footsteps** locked to the walk animation (one step every half second, exactly when a foot lands, alternating feet, slightly different every time; a calm, low heel-then-toe thump rather than a sharp click), a very quiet looping **wind** (a little stronger when he walks into it), and deep **jump**, **landing** and **teleport** sounds (charge, vanish, arrive, fall, heavy landing). The jump / landing / teleport sounds keep their deep bass at normal strength while the air and noise parts are softened. A small room reverb gives them space. The **SND ON/OFF** button in the game bar mutes everything (remembered), and the Android app silences the game while it is in the background. Levels live in the `LEVEL` constants at the top of `tools/sfx.js` (rebuild with `tools/build_html.py`).

- **Settings** (the SET button in the game bar, next to SND): a volume slider from 0 to 200% (100% = the designed level, saved on the device) for MASTER, FOOTSTEPS, WIND, JUMP + LANDING, TELE + BLUE (teleport, Lapse Blue and Max Blue) and ATTACKS. Let go of a slider and you hear that sound (the wind is already playing underneath). The game ignores the controls while the settings are open; Escape / the Android back button closes them.
- **Training dummy:** SPAWN DUMMY in the settings puts an inverted-colour Gojo in front of him, facing him (REMOVE DUMMY or going back to the menu clears him). A hit that reaches him (the hooks and elbow about 15-17 px from Gojo's hips, the kick 26 px) flashes him white, pops up a damage number, plays a body-blow sound and shoves him back (the kick hardest); a swing that falls short just whiffs, so walk up to him first. He has a health bar (300, with a pale trail showing the damage just taken); at zero he is knocked out (topples over, flickers, fades) and a fresh one steps in after a moment.
- **Hit counter and combo meter** (top right): HITS and total damage while you keep landing hits (it restarts after 1.6 s without one), and a 4-pip meter that fills while the hits land in order right hook > left hook > elbow > kick. All four in order adds 25 bonus damage, shows COMBO! and plays a finisher sound. A KO counter sits underneath.
- **Hit reactions:** ten reactions of ten frames each - head snap, doubled over, stagger back, full spin, knocked flat (and gets up), skid on the heels, arms up to guard, hop back, dizzy wobble and drop to one knee - picked at random every time a hit lands, on the dummy *and* on Gojo. They are one set of art in Gojo's own colours, reused inverted for the dummy.
- **Attacking dummy:** SPAWN ATTACKER in the settings (replacing the punching bag) makes the inverted Gojo walk up to you, swing random hits from the combo (with inverted glows: the blue becomes orange) and rest between them. When a swing connects you reel with a random reaction, get knocked back, cannot act until you recover and are untouchable for a moment afterwards. Jumping or teleporting out of the way dodges it. You can fight back: he flinches, can be knocked out, and a new attacker steps in.

- **Lapse Blue** (the BLUE button above-left of ATTACK; keyboard L): *standing*, he blinks forward - folds into blue light, streaks across and re-forms right in front of the enemy (50 px ahead if there is nobody). *Holding the stick to the right (forward) while you press it*, he stands his ground instead: one hand comes out of his pocket, an orb of blue light gathers, and he pulls the enemy in (a quarter second) and stuns it for 0.4 s. Each is 15 frames; there is a short cooldown.
- **Knockback:** hooks and the elbow only nudge the enemy (about 1-2 px) so the whole combo keeps connecting; the roundhouse kick sends him sliding about 15 px with a dust trail along the ground.
- **Crouch** (pull the joystick down; keyboard S / Down / C toggles it): a **deep crouch** (the hips drop 15 px, so his head is about 14 px lower than before) and **nothing can hit him while he is down**, not even the low kick (the attacker's swings miss; he is also safe while going down and standing up). Every crouch animation is **15 frames**: going down (stand-up is the same frames backwards, 0.42 s), the idle (the legs never move, only the head sways), crouch-walk forward and back (60% speed, softer steps), the spring jump out of the crouch (about 30 px high), the crouch punches and the crouch dash. Blocking works crouched too, and Max Blue from a crouch is unchanged.
- **Crouch punching:** the ATTACK button works while crouched too (the button label changes to R.JAB / L.UPPR). Two 15-frame punches alternate, one per press: a low right jab (about 10 damage) and a left uppercut that rises from below (about 13 damage), both at 30 FPS. They reach about 16 px, only nudge the enemy, count as hits on the hit counter, and he stays crouched and in place while throwing them. Press twice quickly to chain them; wait 1.4 s and the combo starts again with the jab.
- **Max Blue** (press BLUE / keyboard L *while crouched*): he stands up first (the 10 crouch frames backwards), then summons **Max Blue** with a 40-frame animation at 20 FPS (2 seconds): the hand comes out of his pocket, a small orb of blue light grows above his palm while a deep-blue outline glows around him, he braces and channels, then releases it.
  - **The orb** is a big glowing sphere in pixel art (a 15-frame loop played at 20 FPS): a white-hot core with wispy veins of light, mid-blue clouds that keep boiling, a dark ragged rim and thin cyan arcs swaying along the edge. It floats at head height in front of him and lives for 3.4 seconds. It dims the sky, drags the wind and about 70 chunks of rooftop debris into itself (the far half of the swirl passes behind the orb, the near half in front, with short motion streaks, glowing blue as they get caught), sends rings across the floor and rumbles the screen faintly.
  - **The enemy is caught by it:** he is lifted off the floor and swung round the orb, tumbling, passing behind it and then in front of it, on a loop that keeps tightening (about 50 px away at first, 28 px once he is deep in) and gets faster, so he is moved around *and* pulled in. He can't be hit while he is caught and his AI is held; the orb grinds him down (about 4-6 damage every quarter second, but it can never finish him). When the orb collapses, the blast flings him off, straightening him up in the air, he lands about 85 px in front of Gojo, takes 25 more damage and plays a hit reaction (and goes down if that was the last of his health).
  - Jump, attack, crouch and Lapse Blue are ignored while he summons it, there is a short cooldown afterwards, you can't start a second orb while one is out, and he can still move and fight while it is. Getting hit during the summon cancels it. Sound: a rising swell, a deep boom as it is released, a humming pull for as long as it lives, and a heavy blast at the end.

- **Dash** (the DASH button next to ATTACK; keyboard K or Shift): an evasive dash of 15 frames at 30 FPS (half a second). He dashes the way you are walking: hold the stick forward and he dashes forward (50 px), hold it back and he dashes back (42 px); standing still is a dash back (the evasive one). It works from the ground, **from a crouch** (the held direction decides and he stays crouched afterwards, with its own low dash animation) and **in mid-air** (jump, push the stick to the side you want, then press DASH; one air dash per jump, and the jump carries on underneath). The 15 frames are a low lunge forward or a leaning-back hop, with blue afterimages and speed streaks behind him and the wind rushing past; he is not hit while the dash is under way (about its first 0.37 s), and attacks, jumps, BLUE and crouching are ignored until it ends. It has a short dust puff on the ground and its own whoosh (on the JUMP + LANDING volume slider).

- **Block** (hold the BLOCK button, top-left of the button block; keyboard: hold B): his signature pose in the same side-view pixel style - the near hand comes out of the pocket and rises in front of his face with the index and middle fingers pointing up, while the other hand stays in his pocket (10 frames: 5 to raise it, 5 for the held pose, and it goes down again the same way when you let go). There is no visible barrier. While the pose is up, the attacking enemy's swings from the **front** are blocked (a BLOCKED popup, a bump of the screen and a small push back, no damage and no reaction); a swing from **behind** him still lands (dash past the attacker and he turns round and hits you in the back). He can **walk, crouch and crouch-walk while blocking**, but he is much slower (30% of his normal speed: about 13 px/s forward, 9 back; crouched about 8 px/s) and has his own 15-frame walking and crouching block animations with the raised hand; attacking, dashing, BLUE and jumping are ignored until you let go, and he cannot block in the air. If he is standing still the hand lowers smoothly when you let go; if he is moving or crouched it just drops.
- **Blocking dummy:** SPAWN BLOCKER in the settings puts an inverted-colour Gojo in front of you in the same raised-hand pose. **Normal punches** (the whole 4-hit combo and the crouch punches) are blocked: no damage, no hit counted, a BLOCKED popup. **Lapse Blue's pull is blockable but still works**: he is dragged to you but is not stunned. **Max Blue is unblockable**: he is caught by the orb and ground down as usual. A punch that reaches him from behind (his guard faces Gojo) lands normally, and he drops his guard while he is reeling from a hit.
- **Buttons:** the four action buttons are smaller than before (about 59 px, ATTACK 66 px, on a 390 px tall phone); the joystick kept its size.

- **Walking combo:** press ATTACK while **walking forward** (stick or arrow held forward) and he throws a different 4-hit combo, 15 frames per hit, each with a small lunge forward (never through the enemy): **1** a quick straight jab with the near hand, **2** a kick forward with the near leg, **3** a straight punch with the other hand, **4** a kick with the other leg (the hardest hit, it sends the enemy sliding with a dust trail). The button names the next hit (JAB, KICK, CROSS, KICK2). Standing still or walking backward is still the normal combo. All four count towards the combo meter in order (the finisher bonus works the same), they are blocked by the blocking dummy like any normal punch, and the hitting hands and legs have a blue glow and a blue outline.

| Path | What it is |
|---|---|
| `index.html` | The whole game, one file, works offline in any browser |
| `dist/JujutsuSorcerers.apk` | Android app (Android 5.0+). Debug-signed, for sideloading |
| `android/` | App wrapper: full-screen landscape WebView. `android/build.sh` rebuilds the APK (needs a JDK, Android SDK `platforms;android-34` and `build-tools;34.0.0`) |
| `art/` | Launcher icon + face art |
| `tools/` | Python scripts that generate the sprite frames and icons (`side.py` for the in-game sprites incl. the jump and the teleport double jump, `sfx.js` for the sound effects, `face.py` for the title art, `icons.py`, then `build_html.py`) |

**Install on Android:** copy the APK to the phone, open it, and allow "install unknown apps" if asked.
The APK uses a throwaway debug key (`android/debug.keystore`); publishing to Google Play needs your own release key.
